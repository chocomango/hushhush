HOW TO PLAY

Blend in. Find the infiltrators.

  1. Pass the phone. Each player privately reveals their secret.
  2. Give clues. Civilians share a word; Imposters get a similar word. Neither group knows which role they have. Mr. White gets no word and must bluff.
  3. Start fairly. Mr. White cannot speak first in the opening round, but may start later rounds.
  4. Vote. Eliminate one player, reveal their role, then continue with the survivors.
  5. Win. Civilians must eliminate every Imposter and Mr. White. The infiltrators win when only one Civilian remains. An eliminated Mr. White wins instantly by guessing the Civilian word.

Choose your pace

Classic: discuss at your own pace. Blitz: 15 seconds each, one clue cycle then vote. Chaos: a fresh clue challenge every round.

Special identities

For 5 or more players, optionally add a Jester or Accomplice. The Jester receives the Imposter word and wins alone when voted out. The Accomplice receives the Civilian word but belongs to the infiltrator team. Both know their special identity.

Civilians must eliminate all Imposters, Mr. Whites, and Accomplices. The Jester is neutral and does not count as an infiltrator; a surviving Jester does not win with either team.

Secret ballots

Enable secret ballots in setup. Pass the phone so each surviving player casts one vote, excluding themselves. Only totals are shown after everyone finishes; individual choices stay private. Ties trigger a runoff among the tied players.

Play fair, have fun

Never say, spell, or translate your secret word. Each surviving player gets one vote; select the player with the most votes. For a tie, discuss and vote again. Eliminated players sit out clues and voting.

Based on the official Undercover pass-and-play rules.

CUSTOM WORDS

Make your own pair.

Add two related—but meaningfully different—words. Their sides will be randomized each game.
