HOW TO PLAY

Blend in. Find the infiltrators.

  1. Pass the phone. Each player privately reveals their secret.
  2. Give clues. Civilians share a word; Imposters get a similar word. Neither group knows which role they have. Mr. White gets no word and must bluff.
  3. Vote. Eliminate one player, reveal their role, then continue with the survivors.
  4. Win. Civilians must eliminate every Imposter and Mr. White. The infiltrators win when only one Civilian remains. An eliminated Mr. White wins instantly by guessing the Civilian word.

Based on the official Undercover pass-and-play rules.
